# Unicode support tests

Test ids refer to `unicode_acceptance_tests.md` in the repository root.

`<ucd-dir>` must hold `UnicodeData.txt`, `EastAsianWidth.txt`, `CaseFolding.txt`,
`PropList.txt` and `DerivedCoreProperties.txt` from <https://www.unicode.org/Public/UCD/latest/ucd/>. Regenerate the
shipped table with `python3 -I dev/mkunitbl.py <ucd-dir>` (it writes `src/eunitbl.h`).

`roundtrip.sh` runs only its own start-up macro, not `me.emf`, so no file hooks or
`translate-buffer` are applied - it tests the engine's own load and save.

Phase 7 build (then `usrc/`, now `src/`), after the port to the original `src` (now `src.old/`): `roundtrip.sh` 37 of 37, `convert.py` 70 of 70, `checks.py` 18 of 18,
`display.py` 47 of 47, `edit.py` 101 of 101, `input.py` 22 of 22, `search.py` 65 of 65,
`macro.py` 78 of 78, `ipipe.py` 21 of 21, `hilight.py` 11 of 11, `spell.py` 46 of 46 (13 in an 8-bit build), `osd.py` 16 of 16,
`codepage.py` 23 of 23 (12 of 12 in `src.old`), `encoding.py` 41 of 41, `bintools.py` 12 of 12 (also in `src.old` and the
8-bit build), `hash.py` 23 of 23 (21 of 21 in the 8-bit build; `src.old` fails the 7 cases changed in October 2026); `editdiff.py` (87 steps), `inputdiff.py` (10 key sequences),
`searchdiff.py` (42 results) and `macrodiff.py` (96 results) find no differences between the
original `src.old`, the ported `src.old`, 8-bit and Unicode builds; `test/ipipe` passes 31 of 31 row checks in the Unicode build. `edit.py` and `search.py` give expected columns as byte
offsets and compare them with `$window-col`, a char index, after converting. In `input.py` backspace is sent as DEL (0x7f), as terminals do.

In a headless macro all commands are one undo step unless separated, so the edit tests put
`-2 undo` between commands as a key press would. Any command between two `undo`s, including
`ml-write`, ends the undo chain, so the next `undo` redoes; the tests use `N undo` instead.

The load message is suppressed while a macro runs, so `display.py` checks it in a pseudo terminal.

Baseline (Oct 2026, 8-bit build): 22 of 37 pass. The 9 UTF-16/32 files and the 2 NUL
files fail as the engine drops NUL bytes; the 4 mixed line-ending files fail as the
endings are "corrected" (see `unicode_design.md` sections 3 and 4).
